# The Aster Room: House Knowledge

## The room
Modern Canadian fine dining, wine-led, 54 seats plus an 8-seat chef's counter and a 14-seat private cellar room ("The Vault").
A downtown room in Western Canada. Executive Chef: Mara Delacroix. Wine Director: on the floor nightly.

## Hours
- Dinner: Tuesday to Saturday, 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM (kitchen closes 10:00 PM)
- Closed Sunday and Monday
- Chef's counter: two seatings, 5:30 PM and 8:30 PM, Thursday to Saturday only

## Menu (current season)
- À la carte, plus a 7-course tasting menu at $165 per person
- Signatures: dry-aged duck with saskatoon jus; bison tartare with juniper; sablefish in koji beurre blanc; wild mushroom agnolotti (vegetarian)
- Tasting menu has vegetarian and pescatarian versions with 48 hours notice
- Dietary: gluten-free easily accommodated; strict allergies handled with advance notice. The kitchen works with nuts, shellfish and dairy.

## Wine program
- About 450 labels, focused on grower Champagne, Burgundy, Northern Rhône, and top Canadian producers (Okanagan, Niagara)
- Grower Champagne is the house calling card. Ask about the current Maison Perrot and Clos Verrier allocations.
- Wine pairing with the tasting menu: $95 classic, $155 cellar reserve
- Corkage: $45 per 750ml bottle, maximum two bottles, not offered on wines already on our list

## House pairing ladders
These are the Wine Director's approved three-glass progressions. Keep the order and exact prices when the named dish is requested, but explain each choice in your own concise voice.
- Bison tartare pairing ladder: Vallon des Pierres, Côtes du Rhône ($16); Ridgeline Estate, Okanagan Syrah ($21); Rousset-Bellay, Gevrey-Chambertin ($42). The juniper and lean raw meat need freshness first, savoury Syrah as the house recommendation, then Burgundy's perfume and fine tannin as the step up.

## Policies
- Dress: smart casual, no sportswear or ball caps
- Children: welcome, though the room is best suited to guests 12 and over
- Cancellation: 24 hours notice; parties of 7 or more require a card and 48 hours notice
- Parking: street after 6 PM; a public lot one block north
- Pets: service animals only
- Gift cards: available online and at the door

## Private dining: The Vault
- Seats 14 (seated) or 20 (standing reception), fully enclosed within the cellar
- Minimum spend: $2,500 Tuesday to Thursday, $4,000 Friday and Saturday
- Set menus from $120 per person; custom wine programs available with the Wine Director

## Reservations
The assistant does not book tables. Reservations are made by the guest through the booking link or by phone, and a host confirms them.
